Family Assistant

A private household assistant for one family.

Family Assistant is a personal app that runs on a home server for two family members. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up.

The two of us message it on Telegram to add items to our grocery list, add tasks to our to-do list, and add events to our family calendar (a self-hosted planner). It can also build our H-E-B online grocery cart from the grocery list; we review and pay in the H-E-B app ourselves.

With our permission it reads our own Gmail inboxes (read-only) to find emails that describe an appointment or event, and asks us in Telegram whether to add each one to our calendar. Nothing is added until one of us approves it.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what data is used and how.

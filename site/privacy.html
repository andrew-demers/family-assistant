Privacy Policy

Family Assistant. Effective November 3, 2026.

Who uses this app

Family Assistant is a private app used only by the two members of one household. It runs on our own home server. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With each user's consent, the app uses the Gmail read-only permission (gmail.readonly) to read recent emails in that user's own inbox. It cannot send, delete, change or label email.

How the data is used

  • The app looks at recent emails (by default from the last two days, excluding promotions and social categories) to find ones that describe a dated event, such as an appointment, reservation or school event.
  • To do this, the text, subject, sender and date of each such email are sent to the Anthropic Claude API, which returns any events the email describes (title, date, time, location). Anthropic processes this data to provide its service under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Each found event is sent to the two household members in a private Telegram chat, showing the event details plus the email's subject and sender, so one of us can choose to add it to our family calendar or skip it.

What is stored

  • On our home server: the IDs of emails already checked (so they are not read twice), and for each proposed event its details plus the email's subject and sender. Full email bodies are not stored.
  • On our home server: the Google access token for each account, kept private.
  • In our self-hosted family calendar: events we choose to add, with a note naming the email's subject and sender.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as needed to provide the features above (the Claude API for finding events, and Telegram for showing them to us). No humans other than the two household members read the emails.

Family Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Either user can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted from the home server on request.
